# v0.1.21 — Plan travel with Ally

Travel now follows three steps: tell Ally about the trip, review your options,
and finalize an itinerary for the human assistant to book. The form captures
trip purpose, separate stay destination and arrival airport, dates and a brief.
Conversation stays beside the options so refinements can refresh the affected
choices while retaining existing selections.

Airline preferences distinguish preferred from required airlines, with clear
messages when the search returns no matching offers. Hotel and restaurant
research offers up to ten sourced suggestions per category, using the actual
stay and dining locations. Suggestions do not guarantee prices or availability.
Test mode clearly identifies sandbox fares and the fictional Duffel Airways.

“Plan a similar trip” reuses saved trip preferences with new dates and a fresh
itinerary; the original trip and its booking history remain intact. Final plans
continue through the saved artifact, reviewed email and HI completion workflow.
EAiOS does not purchase reservations.

Both installer editions include these changes. Core continues to exclude Ticker.
Existing module choices, agents, credentials, custom instructions, trips and
delegation history are preserved. Existing installations can use Settings →
App Settings → Check for updates; fresh Core installations are pinned to v0.1.21.
