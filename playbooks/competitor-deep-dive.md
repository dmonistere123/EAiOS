# Competitor Deep-Dive (swarm)

Runs as a Kanban Swarm v1 graph: parallel workers → verifier → synthesizer.
Draft status — swarm roles currently all map to the default profile until
specialist profiles (scout, quill) exist as real Hermes profiles.

## Swarm plan

- Workers (parallel): product & pricing moves; hiring & leadership signals; customer sentiment & reviews; funding/financial signals.
- Verifier: cross-check every material claim against a second source; discard uncorroborated claims.
- Synthesizer: write the one-page brief — what changed, why it matters to Allygnment, recommended response.

## Steps

1. Confirm the target competitor name with the executive before dispatch.
2. Dispatch the swarm with the goal "Deep-dive brief on <competitor> for Allygnment's CEO".
3. Verifier gate: claims without corroboration are dropped, not hedged.
4. Deliver the cited brief (grounded-citations rules) to the executive.
